### AprilGrid target (verified on the phone video)
- Tag 0 is at the bottom-left; IDs run row-wise left to right, rows going up.
- The tags are printed rotated 180° relative to OpenCV's convention. OpenCV corners
  0..3 are therefore the board's BR, BL, TL, TR (homography fit 1.4 px vs 65–94 px
  for the other orders).
- The measured tag pitch is 59.99 mm (likely a 60 mm design). The full board has not
  been re-measured yet.

### Pipeline stages (CLI `metrogram <stage>`)

| Stage | What it does | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| `calibrate` | AprilGrid/ChArUco; video, images or RAW; pose-diverse frame selection; holdout; outlier rounds; YAML + Kalibr + JSON + plots | synthetic: focal length within 0.04 %; tag-corner floor ≈ 0.2 px |
| `extract-frames` | sharpest frame per segment of a video | — |
| `sfm` | pycolmap 4.2.1: fixed or refined intrinsics; preset downscaling; exhaustive or sequential matching | synthetic: cameras within 34 µm (mean) at 0.45 m |
| `markers` | board detection + per-image plane check + triangulation + robust Umeyama → metric board frame; check points held out; PLY overlays | synthetic: check points 0.10 mm RMS, scale within 36 ppm |
| `dense` | COLMAP undistortion → OpenMVS densify / mesh / [refine] / texture; board-plane check | synthetic: 80 mm cube top at 80.09 mm |
| `view` | Open3D Z-up turntable PNGs or `--show`; overlays; frames on board/objects; texture-preserving crop | renders checked visually |

### Phone video smoke test (`data/raw/xperia1m3_aprilgrid_eis.mp4`; autofocus and EIS on, 1080p)
- Calibration: 1.50 px RMS (holdout 1.53 px). Coverage was only 54 % (the board stayed
  near the image center). The result is a negative example, as expected.
- SfM: 120/120 frames registered, 0.85 px mean reprojection error.
- Markers: check points 0.64 mm RMS (0.30 mm in-plane, 0.56 mm out-of-plane). The
  400 mm diagonal comes out 0.22 mm short.
- Dense (cpu-fast): 4.17 M points, 197k-face textured mesh in 12 min on CPU. Board-plane
  scatter 0.72 mm RMS, offset +1.14 mm.

### OpenMVS
- Built on the laptop with `scripts/build_openmvs.sh`: **v2.3.0** (the Ubuntu 22.04
  release), CGAL 5.4 and VCG, all pinned to commit SHAs and verified. Installed into
  `~/opt/openmvs` (not `~/.local`, to keep CGAL out of the ROS/colcon builds). No sudo.
- v2.4.0 was dropped: it needs OpenCV ≥ 4.11, nanoflann ≥ 1.5 and libjxl.
- Quirks handled in code:
  - TextureMesh's OBJ export segfaults, so the stage exports PLY and converts it to OBJ
    with trimesh.
  - Paths stored in the `.mvs` scene are resolved relative to the working directory,
    so the tools run from `work/`.
  - OpenMVS drops a log file into whatever directory it runs from.
