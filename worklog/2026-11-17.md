### Fixes in the image loading (`sources.py`, `sfm.py`, `markers.py`)
- A folder holding both RAW and JPG files is now read as RAW only (`list_images`).
  Before, the mixed sizes made `calibrate` fail; in `sfm`, a RAW and its JPG
  would have written the same PNG name.
- Images stay in sensor orientation. The RAW decoder's `user_flip=0` and OpenCV's
  `IMREAD_IGNORE_ORIENTATION` stop rotation flags from turning portrait frames.
- RAW frames are cropped to the maker's area (R6: 5472×3648, matching EXIF and the
  device config). LibRaw's own output is 5496×3670. This is `decode_raw`, shared
  by calibration and SfM.
- New test: `tests/test_sources.py`.

### AprilGrid target: two errors found and fixed
1. **Kalibr tags have a 2-bit black border** (10×10 bits); OpenCV assumes 1 bit.
   - On the R6 frames this gave 0 detections.
   - On the 1080p phone video, tags were detected but the corners landed on the
     wrong edge.
   - New config field `border_bits` (default 2).
2. **The board dimensions were wrong.** The printed caption reads
   "6x6 tags, size=5.0cm and spacing=1.0cm", so: tag 50.0 mm, gap 10.0 mm
   (ratio 0.2), pitch 60 mm.
   - A calibration sweep on the 30 cm set has its minimum there: median 0.77 px,
     against about 5 px at 49.5 or 50.5 mm.
   - The old 43.13 mm / 0.391 values were an artefact of error 1.
   - The config is updated. The absolute scale still needs a caliper check.
- **Consequence: the phone-video results of 2026-11-12 (calibration, markers,
  dense) are invalid** because both errors affected them. Re-run if they matter.

### Detector rework (`targets.AprilGridDetector`)
- Detection runs on a copy downscaled to ≤ 2000 px. At full resolution the tags
  are hundreds of pixels wide and OpenCV's thresholding fails.
- Each corner is then refined at full resolution:
  1. subpixel edge points on the middle 60 % of each tag side;
  2. the dark-to-bright transition nearest the coarse edge, searched outward;
  3. a line fit with outlier rejection;
  4. line intersections;
  5. a small cornerSubPix on the X-junction.
- Several simpler versions were tried and rejected:
  - a single cornerSubPix with a window capped at 30 px could not reach coarse
    corners 40–60 px off;
  - a wide-window cornerSubPix was pulled onto the data bits;
  - an edge search taking the outermost transition jumped across the white gap.
- The synthetic renderer now draws the 2-bit border and Kalibr's corner squares.
  All 22 tests pass, the slow ones included.

### Robust holdout statistics and blur filter (`calibration.py`)
- `error_stats` is computed for both train and holdout views:
  - per-view RMS median and p90, per-point error median and p95;
  - a **core RMS** over views at or below the training outlier threshold
    (`reject_factor` × the median train view), so it is comparable to the train RMS.
- The "overfitting / unstable intrinsics" warning now uses the core RMS and the
  median view. Bad holdout views get a separate warning that lists them.
- **Blur filter:** the AprilGrid detector returns a per-tag edge width (step height
  divided by the maximum slope across the tag edges). Points whose edge width
  exceeds max(`--blur-floor-px` 3, `--blur-rel` 2 × the scan median) are dropped
  before view selection.
- Everything is written to `calibration.yaml` (`errors.train`, `errors.holdout`,
  `blur_filter`) and to `report.json` (`n_blurred` and `edge_px_median` per view).
- Tests: edge width grows with blur, the blur filter, outlier isolation in
  `error_stats`. All 25 tests pass.

### R6 calibrations (OPENCV model, `--min-points 12`, blur filter on)

| Set | fx = fy (px) | cx, cy | k1, k2 | train RMS | holdout core RMS | holdout median view | bad holdout views |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 cm | 5859.5 | 2747, 1805 | −0.075, +0.080 | 0.91 px | 0.91 px | 0.63 px | 1/12 (308A4847) |
| 30 cm | 5743.5 | 2756, 1819 | −0.081, +0.072 | 1.66 px | 1.28 px | 1.06 px | 2/12 (308A4785, 4789) |

- **The intrinsics generalize:** the holdout core RMS is no larger than the train RMS.
  The plain holdout RMS (2.6 and 3.0 px) is driven by the 1–2 bad views.
- The focal length changes by 2.0 % between the two focus settings (focus breathing).
- The median edge width is 5.1–5.2 px, so even the good frames are soft. A perfect
  step would give about 1.5–2 px. ISO 1600 noise, f/8 diffraction (Airy disc
  ≈ 1.6 px) and slight defocus all contribute.
  - The blur filter dropped 3.6–5.2 % of the points.
  - The 3 bad holdout views are **not** blurrier than the median (edge widths
    4.7–6.6 px), so their error has another cause, possibly steep obliquity. Open.
- Points on good views are about 0.55–0.7 px (median). At 30 cm that is about
  35 µm on the board.
