### Corner refinement modes and the print-spread finding
- New `--corner-refine full|lines|none` (`AprilGridDetector(corner_refine=)`,
  recorded in `settings` of calibration.yaml). The class docstring was stale
  (described an old two-pass cornerSubPix); rewritten.
- R6 40 cm set:

  | mode | train RMS | holdout RMS | fx |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | full | 0.48 px | 0.49 px | 5630.1 ± 1.3 |
  | lines | 3.36 px | 3.28 px | 5602.4 ± 9 |
  | none | 18.0 px | 18.3 px | 5609 ± 43 |

- Edge-line corners lie 0.31 mm outward along the tag diagonal from the
  junction corners (median of 1104 corners, 11 frames; p5–p95 0.15–0.36 mm,
  ~0 across): black areas printed ≈ 0.22 mm larger per edge (ink/toner
  spread). Tags therefore overlap their corner squares by ≈ 0.44 mm.
- **This corrects the "misregistration" explanation given earlier today:** the
  15 cm rejections come from that overlap (≈ 21 px at 21 µm/px), not from a
  misprint.
- The caliper check must use same-side edges (tag 0 to tag 5 left edges:
  300 mm), not the outer extent (350 mm + spread).
- 15 cm with `--corner-refine lines --min-points 4`: 42 frames, 104 points,

### Target generator usage (explained to the user, no code change)
- The user will first test-print the ChArUco on plain paper (printer scale,
  ink spread, detection), before the matte-paper-on-glass version.
- `target-svg` takes only the YAML, `-o` and `--page a4|a3|letter|fit`
  (landscape; the 8 mm margin is fixed, not exposed). The board geometry
  (squares_x/y, square_size_m, marker_size_m, dictionary) comes from the YAML:
  make one YAML per printed variant.
- When setting the measured square size, scale `marker_size_m` by the same
  factor (keep the 0.75 ratio).
- Bit size on paper: 12 mm squares give 9 mm markers, 7 bits → 1.29 mm per
  bit; with ≈ 0.2 mm spread per edge a white bit shrinks to ≈ 0.85 mm. Squares
  much under ~8 mm are risky on plain printers.
- Other session notes: explained Claude Code's `idleCompaction` setting and
  what ruff does (no project impact).

### Target generator: custom sizes and `target-design`
- `target-svg`: `--page` also takes `WxH` in mm (as given) besides a4 / a3 /
  letter / fit; new `--margin` (default 8 mm) and `--caption full|short|none`.
  The caption font shrinks to 2 mm for narrow boards; a `fit` page is widened
  for the caption and the scale-bar label. The A4 stopgap SVG was regenerated
  (only the caption position changed); rsvg render: 247 / 247 corners.
- New `metrogram target-design` (`src/metrogram/target_design.py`): from a
  device YAML and `--gsd-um`, or `--distance` (+ `--fx`), it sizes a ChArUco:
  - the board is `--fill` (0.6) × the field of view (`--aspect sensor|square`);
  - the square size is the largest of: about 10 squares on the short side
    (rounded down), a marker bit of at least 6 px, and a printed bit of at
    least 0.8 mm (both rounded up to 0.5 mm);
  - it prints a summary and writes the YAML with `-o`.
- R6 examples: 40 cm → 15 × 10 squares of 14.5 mm (217.5 × 145 mm);
  GSD 25 µm (15 cm) → 10 × 7 of 7.5 mm, limited by the printed bit size.
- Tests: `tests/test_target_design.py` (rules, square aspect, error, YAML
  round trip); `test_target_svg.py` + custom page / fit / caption.
- README §7.1, new "Designing and generating a ChArUco target": both
  commands and their options, the aspect-ratio question (sensor aspect for a
  one-camera board, square for a shared board or an SfM base board; fill
  matters more), ordering from a print shop (process, matte opaque material,
  spread compensation), printing it yourself (PDF via rsvg-convert, 100 %),
